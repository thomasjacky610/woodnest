The Chester Sofa

From $1,890

The Chester is the sofa we'd put in our own homes. Eight-way hand-tied spring suspension, kiln-dried hardwood frame with mortise-and-tenon joints, and cushions built from down-wrapped high-resilience foam rated for 50,000+ compressions.

The result holds its shape — not just for the first year, but for the decade that follows. Removable, machine-washable covers mean you maintain the look over time without professional cleaning costs.

Available in two-seat, three-seat, and L-shape corner configurations. Twelve fabric options from natural boucle to performance linen rated at 100,000 rubs.
