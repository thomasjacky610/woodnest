Walnut Sideboard

From $1,180

The Walnut Sideboard brings together two of the most satisfying design elements in furniture: the deep, figured grain of American walnut and the warm metal of brushed brass. The result is a piece that commands a room without overwhelming it.

The carcass is solid timber throughout — no particleboard, no hollow panels. Interior shelving is fully adjustable to accommodate everything from plates to bottles. The three doors close on a soft-close mechanism that makes the piece feel as quality to use as it looks.

Available in 140cm and 160cm widths. Brass hardware can be specified in matte black if preferred.
