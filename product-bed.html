The Elm Bed Frame

From $1,490

The Elm is a bed frame designed around one principle: it should feel as substantial as it looks. The headboard is solid oak — not oak veneer, not painted MDF — and the platform base eliminates the noise and flex that make cheaper frames so frustrating.

The platform design removes the need for a box spring. Your mattress sits on solid timber slats spaced at 5.5cm intervals — the spacing recommended by all major mattress manufacturers for proper support.

Available in king, queen, and double. The headboard comes in natural oil, white oil, or dark oil finish. Base upholstery in six fabric options.
