The dining table is where families gather every day — it handles hot dishes, spilled drinks, school projects, and everything between. WoodNest dining furniture is built for this reality: solid timber throughout, not veneer on MDF which expands and eventually delaminates. Joints are mortise-and-tenon. Surfaces are finished with a hard-wearing oil that can be refreshed at home when needed. These are tables and chairs designed to get better with use, not worse.

Common Questions

About Dining Furniture

  • Solid wood is cut from a single log or edge-glued sections of solid timber. It moves seasonally with humidity but can be sanded, re-oiled, and repaired indefinitely. Veneer is a thin slice of real wood (0.6–2mm) applied over MDF. It looks similar initially but cannot be sanded or meaningfully repaired if scratched, and MDF swells permanently with moisture exposure.
  • Use heat pads for hot dishes — no finish fully resists direct contact with hot cookware. Use felt pads or coasters under anything that sits on the surface permanently. For oil-finished tables, re-oil every 12–18 months to maintain water resistance. Wipe spills immediately — don't let moisture sit on the surface.
