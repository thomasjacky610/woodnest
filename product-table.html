Oak Dining Table

From $1,240

A solid wood dining table is one of the most important furniture purchases you will make — it handles hot dishes, spilled wine, homework, and daily family use. The WoodNest Oak Dining Table is built for this reality.

Solid European ash throughout — not veneer on MDF. Joints are mortise-and-tenon, the same method used in furniture built to last centuries. The hard oil finish penetrates rather than sitting on the surface, which means it handles daily contact and can be refreshed at home.

Available in fixed 6-seat, fixed 8-seat, or an extending version that adds 50cm via a butterfly leaf mechanism. The leaf is internal — there's nothing separate to store.
